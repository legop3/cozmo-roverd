Get Cozmo rolling.

Connect your robot’s Wi-Fi, pick an MRR server, and give Cozmo a name. No terminal commands or hand-editing configuration files required after installation.

1

Choose a Wi-Fi adapter

This adapter will be dedicated to Cozmo. Your normal internet/MRR connection stays untouched.

Adapters carrying the computer's default network route are protected and cannot be selected.

Wi-Fi is unavailable.
2

Find Cozmo

Put Cozmo in Wi-Fi connection mode so its network and password appear on the robot's screen.

Select an adapter, then scan. Cozmo networks will appear first.

The password is stored only on this computer.

Cozmo's Wi-Fi usually has no internet access. That's expected: MRR stays connected over your computer's other network connection.
3

Connect to MRR

Enter your existing MultiRoombaRover server. Cozmo will appear in its rover list.

Use ws:// or wss:// and include /rover. Use the hostname or IP your Fedora computer can reach.

4

Customize the rover

Good defaults are selected already. Change anything you like.
